# Terms of Use for Rainfall Data

## 1. Open Data

Rainfall observations presented on this dashboard are made available under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**.

You are free to:
- Share and redistribute the data in any format or medium.
- Copy, modify and adapt the data.
- Use the data for any purpose, including commercial applications.

No prior permission is required, provided that appropriate attribution is given.

## 2. Attribution

When using, reproducing or redistributing data obtained through this dashboard, please acknowledge the source:

- **Rainfall observations:** Credit the original weather station or contributor, where identifiable, and reference this dashboard as the source of the data.
- **Third-party data sources:** Where observations originate from external weather platforms or services, please also acknowledge the original platform and, where possible, the individual station.
- **Figures and visualisations:** When reproducing or sharing maps, charts, graphs or other visual material from this dashboard, please acknowledge https://web.csag.uct.ac.za/monitoring/okavango/rainfall and include a link to the original website or page.

A link to this dashboard is appreciated whenever the data or visualisations are shared.


## 4. Third-party Data

The CC BY 4.0 licence applies only to material for which the dashboard operators have the necessary rights to grant such a licence. It does not override third-party rights or licensing conditions.

# Terms of use of Inundation Maps and Derived Products

The inundation maps presented on this dashboard are derived from MODIS satellite observations using a methodology developed and published by Piotr Wolski and colleagues in:

Wolski, P., Murray-Hudson, M., Thito, K., & Cassidy, L. (2017). Keeping it simple: Monitoring flood extent in large data-poor 
wetlands using MODIS SWIR data. International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation, 57, 224–234. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2017.01.005

The derived inundation maps and associated products are made available under the **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC 4.0)**.

You are free to:
- Share and redistribute the maps and derived products.
- Reproduce and incorporate them into reports, publications and presentations.
- Adapt and build upon the products for research, educational and other non-commercial purposes.

Appropriate attribution to the dashboard and the original scientific publication is required.

Commercial use requires separate permission from the relevant rights holder.

The underlying MODIS satellite observations remain subject to their respective data policies and are not relicensed under this provision.
